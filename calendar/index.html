❓ How this calendar works

There is one calendar, and it is on your myAI box. Every device you sign in on (phone, laptop, desktop) shows that same calendar and keeps a copy of it, so it opens instantly and works without a connection. What you change offline is kept on the device and sent to the box as soon as it can be; the dot under ＋ New event says how that stands.

🔔 Reminders you can rely on

For reminders on your phone, also add this calendar to the phone's own calendar app (Settings → Connect your phone). Its alarms are kept on the phone and go off on time even without a connection or with the box switched off. This app shows reminders while it is open; reminders from this app while it is closed come with a later build.

🔁 Changes on two devices

If the same event is changed on two devices before they could sync, the change made later wins on both, and the other is listed under Settings → Recent sync problems, so nothing changes without a trace. An event changed on one device and deleted on another is kept, with the change.

💾 Backups

Every event is a plain .ics file in the box's input/calendar/ folder, which the box's own backups copy with everything else. Settings → ⬇ .ics downloads a calendar as one file any calendar app can open, and Settings → Import brings in an export from Google, Apple or Outlook. If many events disappear from the box at once (a disk replaced without its backup, say), each device keeps a copy and offers to put them back.

🔒 Privacy

The app talks only to your box, on the same address it was opened from. Each person signs in with their own account and sees only their own calendars. Signing out removes this device's copy and its saved password.

⬇️ Updates

The version under ❓ is the build this page is running. A newer build is downloaded in the background and offered there (this build › the new build); it takes over when you tap it, or the next time the app is started.
