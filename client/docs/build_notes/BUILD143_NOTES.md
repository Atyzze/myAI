# myAI Build 143

Twice, on a Galaxy S21 with the app installed from Brave, the whole app closed the moment Stop was pressed on a recording of three to five minutes. On reopening it found the recording unfinished and, after a wait, recovered it. Nothing in myAI closes the app or asks to clear a cache, and a crash of the page alone would normally leave a crash page in the window; the browser itself went away. This build cannot stop a browser from crashing. It makes sure that when one does, at Stop or at any other moment, nothing more than the last seconds of audio can be lost, and that the recording comes back as soon as the app is opened. And since an installed app has no console to look at, it adds a system log, on by default and opened from ❓, so that the next time something like this happens there is a record of what the app was doing up to the moment it died. Database version stays at 13; the log lives in a database of its own.

## What the person sees

- A recording left unfinished by a browser that crashed or was killed is recovered as soon as the app opens again. It no longer shows "Saving in another tab…" and waits for its heartbeat to go stale, and Recover now works at once.
- Such a recording carries a note on its row: "myAI was closed while it was stopping this recording (letting go of the microphone and the encoder). Its audio had been saved by then and was put together from the saved pieces." or "…while it was recording, before it could save the recording itself… at most the last few seconds of audio are missing." The ✕ on the note removes it from the recording.
- A live transcript of a few minutes is saved every minute it changes. Before, after its first minute it was saved again only once it had grown by 4,000 characters or five minutes had passed, so for a recording of a few minutes a browser that died at Stop could take almost all of it.
- Hiding the app during a recording, switching to another app or turning the screen off, saves the audio and the live transcript held in memory straight away.
- ❓ has a new **🧾 Open system logs** button under Check for update. It shows, oldest first, what the app did: how it started (build, installed or tab, browser, cores, memory, screen, storage and whether it is persistent), console messages, errors, the page being hidden, frozen or closed, the network, the screen wake lock, the database connection, each recording's start, microphone and audio context, recovery, and every step of Stop with the page's memory. Copy, Download and Clear sit above it. It keeps the newest 5,000 entries across reloads and crashes.
- When a page load ended without closing normally, the next one starts its log with a warning saying so, with the last thing the earlier one logged, and whether the app was on screen (most likely a crash) or in the background (likelier the phone ending the browser).

## How

- Stop saves first and lets go after. Its first step stores the audio since the last piece (`requestData()` on the encoder, or the PCM in memory written as a piece) and the live transcript; only then are the encoder, the microphone and the audio graph released. The last audio is stored before Stop waits up to 2.5 s for the live transcript to finish, instead of after.
- `stopRecordingNow` writes the step it has reached to the recording's beat before each step begins (`stopStep`: securing, releasing, liveTail, noting, closing, saving; `STOP_STEPS` in `capture-health-core.js`). A Stop that ends without saving, on a full disk say, writes `ended`. Recovery turns the last beat into `rec.interruption` (`interruptionFromBeat`), which `gui.js` shows with `describeInterruption` until `dismissInterruption` removes it; `ended` and a retried failed save are not reported.
- `lockProvesOtherTabsGone()` in `recording-lock.js`: with Web Locks, every tab that records, saves, recovers or deletes holds the recording lock, and the browser releases it when the tab is gone. A tab holding it with nothing else of its own holding it therefore knows no tab is at work on any recording, so `recoverIncompleteRecordings` recovers an unfinished one at once, whatever its heartbeat, lease or finalizer say, and `claimFinalizer` takes over a finalizer left by a dead page. It is asked again for every recording, so a recording this tab starts meanwhile brings the heartbeat rules back; the localStorage fallback proves nothing and keeps them.
- `secureRecordingNow()` runs the saving step during a recording on `visibilitychange` to hidden, `freeze` and `pagehide`, single-flight, and not while Stop or a start is running.
- `liveSnapshotDue` saves a transcript of at most `LIVE_SNAPSHOT_SMALL_CHARS` (32,000 characters) every minute it changed; longer ones keep the growth rule of Build 126.
- The system log: `system-log-boot.js` is the first import of `main.js` and calls `installSystemLog`, which wraps `console.log/info/warn/error`, listens for `error`, `unhandledrejection` and the page's lifecycle events, and opens `myai-system-log` (`entries`, `meta`). `createDebugLog` buffers entries, writes them in one transaction about once a second and a warning or an error at once, trims to `LOG_MAX_ENTRIES`, and keeps one `meta` record per session. Each session holds the Web Lock `myai-log-session-<id>` for its life; `pagehide` marks it ended. On install, every earlier session whose lock is gone (or, without Web Locks, that has not written for ten minutes) is reported if it never ended, with its last entry (`describeUnclosedSession`), and its record removed. Stop logs each step and waits up to 400 ms (`flushSystemLog`) for it to be on disk. `logs-view.js` draws the dialog; `debug-log-core.js` holds the text rules (an error keeps its first three stack frames, a message at most 2,000 characters).

## Tests

`pure`: a few-minute transcript is saved every minute and never more often, a long one still waits; the beat names Stop's step; a beat left during Stop or recording becomes the right interruption and text, and `ended` none. `storage-paths`: a recording whose browser died during Stop a moment ago (fresh row, beat and lease) is recovered at once and keeps the step; so is one this same tab left before a reload with its finalizer; while another tab holds the lock nothing is touched; while this tab holds it for its own work the beat decides, as before; hiding the app stores two seconds of PCM as the next piece and the live transcript; at the moment Stop lets go of the microphone, the last audio and the live transcript are on disk and the beat says `releasing`; the full-disk Stop keeps its flags on every beat written during the slow save, and its recovery is not reported as a closed app. `app-behaviour`: the note is shown with its step, ✕ removes it from the stored recording and the row. `user-journeys`: in Chromium, a recording whose browser died during Stop is recovered at once with all its audio and the note, ✕ removes it; hiding the app during an Opus recording stores a piece straight away; the beat journey now holds the lock for work of its own.

System log: `pure` checks how an error, an object and a long message read, the line format and session breaks, trimming, and the cut-off note (on screen, in the background, ended normally). `app-behaviour` writes to the real log module over the in-memory database: entries logged before it opens are kept, a warning is on disk at once, the next page load reports one cut off with its last entry, a page that ended normally or is still open in another tab is not reported, only the newest entries are kept, Clear empties it, a console warning lands in it, and Open system logs shows it, Clear asks first and ✕ closes it. `storage-paths`: when Stop lets go of the microphone, the log on disk already says so. `user-journeys`: in Chromium, ❓ → 🧾 shows how the app started, the recording, its save on hiding and all five steps of its Stop, from the log's own database, and Escape closes it.

Eighteen new mutation guards. For the log: a Stop step not waited for, console messages not taken, a warning waiting for the timer, a cut-off session not reported, an open tab reported as cut off, the log never trimmed, and an error losing where it was thrown. For the rest: the lock never proving anything, the lock proving it while this tab is busy, a dead finalizer blocking, Stop letting go before saving, the saving step skipping the audio or the live transcript, the short-transcript rule removed, the interruption not noted, a Stop that ended reported as a closed app, the note not shown, and ✕ not removing it. Two guards follow the beat's new `stopStep`.
