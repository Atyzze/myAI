❓ How this app works

Reading this build...

Record voice notes in your browser. Recordings and text are stored in this browser. Transcription sends audio to your /transcribe server and replies send text to your /ollama server; the app contacts no other service.

🔒 Keep what matters

Clearing browsing data deletes every recording and transcript, and a private window keeps nothing. The browser can also evict storage when space runs low. Use ⬇️ on a recording or 📦 Download backup in Settings: a downloaded file is the only copy that cannot disappear.

⏳ Automatic deletion

Audio and text are deleted when they reach the ages set in Settings, one month each by default. Each recording shows the time left, and the first deletion is asked for first.

🎛️ Buttons

📋 beside Start Recording begins a recording that remembers whatever is on the clipboard: the pasted text is attached as context and goes to the AI with the note, exactly like 💬 Continue does with an earlier recording. If the browser will not hand over the clipboard, or it is empty, a box opens to paste or type into instead, holding up to 100,000 characters with line breaks intact. During a recording, 📋 stays where it is and adds whatever is on the clipboard to that recording as one more context item, so something you find halfway through still reaches the AI reply; the live row says how many items the recording carries. It appears in the note's context block, where it can be inspected or removed: tap its text to read it whole, and the 📋 beside it copies the whole text back to the clipboard.

⬇️ Download saves the recording as a file. 🗑️ Delete removes it. 💬 Continue records a follow-up that remembers this note. 🔗 Link bundles several recordings for one follow-up. The small 🗑️ beside ▶ deletes only the audio and keeps the text.

🟢 LIVE and tabs

The recording in progress shows a LIVE badge. Only one tab records at a time; other tabs show LIVE · OTHER TAB. After Stop the recording says Saving… until it is saved. If a tab is closed during a recording, any tab still open recovers what it stored, within a minute and without a reload; until then the recording offers Recover now and Delete.

📝 Live transcription

📝 beside Start Recording starts a recording with live transcription on, whatever the setting says; 📋 on the other side starts one with the clipboard as context (or, during a recording, adds to it), and the big button starts a plain one that follows the setting. During a recording, 📝 shows or hides the transcript a few seconds behind speech; tap the waveform for fullscreen; Settings can draw it less often, or not at all, to save battery. Italic text is the sentence still being spoken. If the connection drops, audio waits in order and is sent when it returns. If the server keeps failing on a few seconds of audio while it answers the rest, that part is left out here with a note. With Auto-transcribe on, every part the live transcript missed is transcribed after the recording; with it off, the saved transcript marks each such part [not transcribed live] and the recording offers 📝 Fill gaps, which sends only those parts. The live transcript is saved with the recording as it grows, at least every five minutes, and transcribing after recording only sends the parts it missed. Cleanup pass in Settings rereads the whole recording.

A phrase repeated where two audio windows overlap is re-listened to, and changed only when the second listen clearly hears it once.

🎤 Is it really recording?

The recorder counts the audio that actually arrives, not the seconds on the clock. If sound stops reaching it, for any reason, a red line appears above the list within four seconds and the recording is marked incomplete. The recording is never stopped by that warning, and it clears itself when sound comes back. So a screen with no warning on it means audio really is being captured.

🗂️ Two tabs, two versions

Updates never delete your notes. A release that changes how notes are stored only adds what it needs and leaves everything already saved in place. If a newer version opens while another tab is recording or still working on a note, that tab goes first: the newer version waits, and starts by itself as soon as the recording, and anything it started such as automatic transcription, has finished.

⬇️ Updates

The version under ❓ is the build this page is running. Tap it to check the server for a newer one. When one is ready the version reads this build › the new build; tap it again to load it. A version shown on its own is always the code actually running, so it never needs a second refresh. Nothing reloads by itself, and nothing reloads at all while this tab is recording, saving a recording, transcribing, writing a reply or making a backup. The second tap then says so; tap once more when that work has finished.

If a browser keeps serving its own cached copy of the app and refuses the new build, the second tap clears the installed shell and loads it from the server. Recordings, transcripts and settings are not touched by that.

💾 Space

The audio counter shows saved and in-progress audio in megabytes. Each recording bar shows its share. While recording, the line below it estimates how much longer this note can run from free space.

Your data lives in this browser
Recordings and text are stored by this browser, on this device. Clearing your browsing data or site data deletes all of it. A private or incognito window keeps nothing at all once it is closed. The browser may also evict this storage on its own when the device runs low on space, and automatic deletion below removes things on a schedule. A downloaded backup is the only copy that survives any of that.
One ZIP with every recording, plus transcripts.json with all text.
Rereads the whole recording in 60-second windows after it ends, fixing what 4-second live windows get wrong.
Automatic deletion
Audio ages from when its recording ended, text from when it was generated; a transcript stays as long as a reply written from it does. Nothing is kept forever: download anything you want to keep. A month is 30 days, a year 365.
Shortening either window deletes immediately. These are not rules that only apply to future recordings: choosing 5 minutes means everything older than 5 minutes is overdue the moment you confirm, and the next sweep removes it - months of recordings, in one step, with no undo. You will be shown exactly how many and how far back before anything happens, and offered a backup first; after a backup, nothing is deleted until you confirm the ZIP was saved.
