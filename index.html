❓ How this app works

Record voice notes in your browser. Audio and generated text are stored locally. AI processing is server-only: transcription sends audio chunks to your configured /transcribe service, and replies send transcript/context text to your configured /ollama service. Server-side transcription backups are optional and disabled by default.

🔒 Browser storage and server processing

Recordings are saved in this browser's storage. The browser can clear that storage if you clear browsing data or it runs low on space, so a recording here is not permanent. The application contains no browser AI models and does not contact Hugging Face or a model CDN.

⚠️ To keep a recording, download it

Press ⬇️ Download to save a recording as a file on your device. That downloaded file is the only permanent copy. Anything you have not downloaded can disappear when the browser reclaims space.

🟢 LIVE and multiple tabs

The active note stays in the recording list with a green border, a ticking timer, and a LIVE badge. Only one tab can record or finalize at a time. Other tabs show LIVE · OTHER TAB and cannot start a second stream until the owning tab stops or its crashed-session lease expires.

🎛️ The buttons under each recording

⬇️ Download - save this recording as a file on your device.
🗑️ Delete - permanently remove this recording from the app.
💬 Continue - record a follow-up that remembers this note, so your recordings can build on each other.
🔗 Link - select several recordings to bundle them together for a single follow-up.

💾 Space

The audio counter shows the combined size of saved recordings and in-progress audio chunks. It always uses megabytes with two decimal places. Each recording bar shows its share of saved audio.

⚙️ Settings
